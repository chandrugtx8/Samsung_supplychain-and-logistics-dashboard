# Samsung_supplychain-and-logistics-dashboard

Supply Chain Performance & Logistics Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline
Supply Chain Performance & Logistics Dashboard | Power BI
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze end-to-end supply chain performance across shipments, inventory, procurement, production, and sales.
2. Short Description / Purpose
The Supply Chain Performance & Logistics Dashboard provides a consolidated view of key logistics and supply chain KPIs across multiple operational areas.
The dashboard is designed to help users monitor shipment performance, carrier efficiency, inventory health, procurement trends, production quality, and sales performance through interactive visuals and drill-down analysis.
It is intended for supply chain analysts, logistics teams, procurement teams, operations managers, and business users who need a clear view of operational performance and potential areas of risk.
3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
- Power BI Desktop – Main platform used to build the dashboard and interactive reports.
- Power Query – Used for data loading, cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures and supply chain KPIs.
- Data Modeling – Relationships between fact and dimension tables were used to support filtering, aggregation, and drill-down analysis.
- CSV Files – Used as the primary source for shipment, inventory, procurement, production, sales, product, customer, facility, and supplier data.
- PBIX Format – Used for dashboard development and reporting.
4. Data Source
The project uses a structured supply chain dataset containing multiple operational tables covering:
- Shipments
- Inventory
- Procurement
- Production
- Sales
- Products
- Customers
- Facilities
- Suppliers
- Date information
The dataset represents a simulated business environment and is used to demonstrate supply chain analytics and Power BI reporting.
The data includes information such as shipment volumes, delivery status, shipping cost, stock levels, reorder points, safety stock, procurement cost, lead time, production output, defects, and sales performance.
5. Features / Highlights
Business Problem
Supply chain data is often spread across different operational areas, making it difficult to quickly understand overall performance.
Important questions such as:
- Which carriers have the highest number of delayed shipments?
- How well are shipments meeting on-time delivery targets?
- Are products overstocked or at risk of replenishment issues?
- How are procurement costs and lead times changing?
- What is the production defect rate?
- How are revenue and profitability changing over time?
can be difficult to answer from raw operational data.
Goal of the Dashboard
The goal of the dashboard is to provide a centralized analytical view of supply chain operations that:
- Tracks logistics and shipment performance.
- Monitors inventory and replenishment risks.
- Evaluates procurement cost and lead-time trends.
- Reviews production output and quality.
- Monitors sales and profitability.
- Enables drill-down analysis by carrier, product, facility, and customer.
Walkthrough of Key Dashboard Areas
Shipment Performance
The shipment dashboard analyzes transportation and delivery performance across carriers.
Key metrics include:
- Total shipments
- Units shipped
- On-time delivery
- Delayed shipments
- In-transit shipments
- Shipping cost
- Average transit time
- Cost per unit shipped
The dashboard enables carrier-level comparison to identify service and transportation-cost differences.
Inventory Management
The inventory dashboard monitors stock position and replenishment-related KPIs.
Key measures include:
- Current stock level
- Safety stock
- Reorder point
- Inventory turnover
- Stock coverage days
- Days of inventory
- Overstock items
- Understock items
These metrics help identify excess inventory and potential replenishment risks.
Procurement Analysis
The procurement section tracks purchasing activity and cost trends.
Key KPIs include:
- Total procurement cost
- Order quantity
- Average unit cost
- Average lead time
- Procurement cost growth
This provides visibility into procurement spending and purchasing performance over time.
Production Performance
The production dashboard evaluates manufacturing output and product quality.
Key metrics include:
- Total units produced
- Defective units
- Defect rate
- Quality yield
- Production performance by product and facility
Sales Performance
The sales dashboard provides visibility into revenue, profitability, and customer-level performance.
Key measures include:
- Gross revenue
- Net revenue
- Total cost
- Total profit
- Profit margin
- Revenue growth
- Sales performance across customers and products
6. Key Findings
Some of the main observations identified from the dashboard include:
- More than 8K shipments and 3M units shipped were analyzed across multiple carriers.
- Carrier performance differed across delayed shipments, on-time delivery, transit performance, and shipping cost.
- Inventory KPIs showed areas of excess stock, while the current dataset did not highlight major understock situations.
- Procurement performance was tracked using lead time, purchasing cost, and order-volume trends.
- Production performance was monitored using defect rate and quality yield.
- Sales performance was analyzed using revenue, cost, and profitability measures.
7. Business Impact
The dashboard demonstrates how supply chain data can be transformed into actionable operational insights.
Potential business applications include:
- Carrier Performance Management: Compare logistics providers based on delivery reliability, delays, transit time, and cost.
- Inventory Planning: Monitor safety stock, reorder points, stock coverage, and excess inventory.
- Procurement Monitoring: Track procurement cost and lead-time trends to support purchasing decisions.
- Production Monitoring: Identify changes in production output, defect rates, and quality performance.
- Operational Reporting: Provide logistics and supply chain teams with a centralized Power BI reporting view.
- Decision Support: Enable users to drill down into products, carriers, facilities, and customers to investigate operational issues.
