# Supply Chain Performance & Logistics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze end-to-end supply chain performance across shipments, inventory, procurement, production, and sales.

## Project Overview

The Supply Chain Performance & Logistics Dashboard provides a consolidated view of key logistics and supply chain KPIs across multiple operational areas.

The dashboard helps users monitor shipment performance, carrier efficiency, inventory health, procurement trends, production quality, and sales performance through interactive visuals and drill-down analysis.

It is intended for supply chain analysts, logistics teams, procurement teams, operations managers, and business users who need a clear view of operational performance and potential areas of risk.

## Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** – Main platform used to build the dashboard and interactive reports.
- **Power Query** – Used for data loading, cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create calculated measures and supply chain KPIs.
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between fact and dimension tables support filtering, aggregation, and drill-down analysis.
- **CSV Files** – Primary source for shipment, inventory, procurement, production, sales, product, customer, facility, and supplier data.
- **PBIX Format** – Used for dashboard development and reporting.

## Data Source

The project uses a structured supply chain dataset containing operational tables for:

- Shipments
- Inventory
- Procurement
- Production
- Sales
- Products
- Customers
- Facilities
- Suppliers
- Date information

The dataset represents a simulated business environment and is used to demonstrate supply chain analytics and Power BI reporting.

The data includes information such as shipment volumes, delivery status, shipping cost, stock levels, reorder points, safety stock, procurement cost, lead time, production output, defects, and sales performance.

## Business Problem

Supply chain data is often spread across different operational areas, making it difficult to quickly understand overall performance.

Key questions include:

- Which carriers have the highest number of delayed shipments?
- How well are shipments meeting on-time delivery targets?
- Are products overstocked or at risk of replenishment issues?
- How are procurement costs and lead times changing?
- What is the production defect rate?
- How are revenue and profitability changing over time?

## Dashboard Goals

The dashboard is designed to:

- Track logistics and shipment performance
- Monitor inventory and replenishment risks
- Evaluate procurement cost and lead-time trends
- Review production output and quality
- Monitor sales and profitability
- Enable drill-down analysis by carrier, product, facility, and customer

## Dashboard Sections

### Shipment Performance

Key metrics include:

- Total shipments
- Units shipped
- On-time delivery
- Delayed shipments
- In-transit shipments
- Shipping cost
- Average transit time
- Cost per unit shipped

This section supports carrier-level comparison of delivery reliability, delays, and transportation cost.

### Inventory Management

Key metrics include:

- Current stock level
- Safety stock
- Reorder point
- Inventory turnover
- Stock coverage days
- Days of inventory
- Overstock items
- Understock items

This section helps identify excess inventory and potential replenishment risks.

### Procurement Analysis

Key metrics include:

- Total procurement cost
- Order quantity
- Average unit cost
- Average lead time
- Procurement cost growth

This section provides visibility into purchasing performance and procurement cost trends.

### Production Performance

Key metrics include:

- Total units produced
- Defective units
- Defect rate
- Quality yield
- Production performance by product and facility

### Sales Performance

Key metrics include:

- Gross revenue
- Net revenue
- Total cost
- Total profit
- Profit margin
- Revenue growth
- Sales performance by customer and product

## Key Findings

- More than **8K shipments and 3M units shipped** were analyzed across multiple carriers.
- Carrier performance varied across delayed shipments, on-time delivery, transit performance, and shipping cost.
- Inventory analysis highlighted areas of excess stock and replenishment risk.
- Procurement performance was monitored through lead time, purchasing cost, and order-volume trends.
- Production performance was tracked using defect rate and quality yield.
- Sales performance was analyzed using revenue, cost, and profitability measures.

## Business Applications

- **Carrier Performance Management** – Compare logistics providers based on delivery reliability, delays, transit time, and cost.
- **Inventory Planning** – Monitor safety stock, reorder points, stock coverage, and excess inventory.
- **Procurement Monitoring** – Track procurement cost and lead-time trends.
- **Production Monitoring** – Review production output, defect rates, and quality performance.
- **Operational Reporting** – Provide a centralized supply chain reporting view.
- **Decision Support** – Drill down by product, carrier, facility, and customer to investigate operational issues.
